# Product requirements

## MVP payment scope

**Status:** OUT OF SCOPE — MVP  
**Date:** 12 Oct 2026

The business owner has not confirmed whether reservation deposits or online payments are needed. The application serves customers primarily in Basilan, Philippines. The MVP booking experience must stay simple and accessible, and must not require customers to pay online.

### Booking workflow

Customers submit booking requests. Every request stays pending until the business owner approves or declines it. Approval does not depend on payment.

### Pricing

Service prices may be displayed when the business owner has configured them. Displaying a price is not payment collection.

### Excluded from the MVP

- Payment gateways
- GCash or Maya integrations
- Online deposits
- Payment collection forms
- Payment screenshot uploads
- Payment verification
- Refund processing
- Payment transaction records
- Payment-related booking statuses

### Later payment work

Future payment functionality requires explicit business owner approval and a separate design review. Do not add payment behavior, schemas, or dependencies before that review.
